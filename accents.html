Palettes

Five complete palettes — header, reading area, footer, accent — each in the three reading modes. Every one is shown on the real interlinear, Alma 32:33, so the choice is made by looking rather than by reading hex values.

What is held constant
  • Sepia is a light theme. Warm paper, dark ink. A warm dark ground is the trap — positive polarity is what reads over long stretches.
  • Paper and chrome are different materials. The bars are never the same value as the page.
  • The accent is a mark — verse numbers, the chapter rule, links, the active view. It never tints the reading ground.
  • All 105 colour pairs clear WCAG AA; body text clears AAA at 7:1.
