Accent templates

Six ways to add one colour to the reader. The base does not change: black words on white, because positive polarity reads better over long stretches. Each template is shown on the real interlinear — Alma 32:33 — in light and dark.

The rule these follow An accent is a mark, never a fill.
  • It marks: verse numbers, the active view, the chapter rule, links, focus.
  • It never tints the reading ground or the body text.
  • Each value must clear 4.5:1 against the surface it sits on — measured live below, not asserted.

Ink

The current setting. No accent at all — structure carries everything.

on white on black Nothing competes with the text. Also nothing guides the eye.
