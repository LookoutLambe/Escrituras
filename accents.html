Palettes

Ten complete palettes — header, reading area, footer, accent — each in the three reading modes, each on the real interlinear (Alma 32:33). Pick by looking, not by reading hex values.

What is held constant
  • Sepia is a light theme. Warm paper, dark ink. A warm dark ground is the trap — positive polarity is what reads over long stretches.
  • Paper and chrome are different materials. The bars are never the same value as the page.
  • The accent is a mark — verse number, chapter rule, link, active view. It never tints the reading ground.
  • Neutrals are shared by all ten; only chrome and accent change. Ten finishes of one system.
  • All 210 pairs clear WCAG AA and body text clears AAA at 7:1 — checked before any CSS was written.
